## Cross-module audit

`--cross-module` is implemented and fixture-tested. It applies the 17 local rules per module, streams successfully parsed graphs into TDB2, and runs four integration rules over the union:

- duplicate identifiers;
- unresolved internal references;
- unresolved/untyped event participants; and
- birth/death chronology.

Full-corpus union attempts remained CPU-bound beyond 20 minutes and were stopped without writing a partial report or baseline. The queries were improved during this pass to use one global target, grouped duplicate identifiers, aggregated warning contexts, and distinct candidate IRIs before existence checks. Even with those changes, a complete union baseline was not obtained in the available performance budget.

Operationally, module-local validation is the normal corpus regression path. Full union validation is an opt-in audit and should receive query-specific ARQ profiling before becoming a scheduled CI gate. This limitation does not affect the fixture-based cross-module tests or the completed local baseline required by ADR-001.

## Review policy

The baseline is not a declaration that its findings are correct data. It records known results so new violations can fail regression checks independently of existing cleanup work. Remove an entry only when the corresponding source issue is fixed or the rule/severity decision is deliberately changed and reviewed.
